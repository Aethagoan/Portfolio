About me

When working on my projects, I often find that the time flies. Code, craft, fix, watch my work function, make improvements; I can do it for hours, and enjoy it. That's what drew me to software, problem solving, trial and error, the thought and effort behind it, watching functions working perfectly. Whether it is crushing a coding competition, grabbing useful data out of a spreadsheet, being nice to look at, gathering information, I strive to see all of my projects reach their potential and end goals.

Life isn't all about work. I seek a functional balance between productivity and being with my partner and family. I want to build friendships in my workplace and do what I can to care for those around me. I seek to work for a place that understands that life is hard, that seeks to support me, just as I will support them.

I've had access to a computer since before I could read. I started learning Python before I was 15. I've continued in that trend, picking up skills and functional knowledge early in my path. I've used this knowledge to help where I can. I've fixed wifi configurations, set up port forwarding, learned to use technologies like TailScale, and helped others through nontrivial web errors and computer problems. I want to be the best engineer that I can. When I find problems, I fix them.
