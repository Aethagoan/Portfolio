About me

When working on my projects, I often find that the time flies. Code, craft, fix, watch my work function, make improvements, I can do it for hours, and enjoy it. That's what drew me to software - I like problem solving, I like trial and error, I like putting effort in. Watching my work do it's proper job, whether that's crushing a coding competition, grabbing useful data out of a spreadsheet, being nice to look at, gathering information, I strive to see my all my projects reach their potential and end goals.

Life isn't all about work. I seek a functional balance between productivity for any means, and being with my partner and family. I want to build friendships in my workplace and do what I can to care for those around me. I seek to work for a place that understands that life is hard, that seeks to support me, just as I will support them.
