Competition project

SpaceWars

A C# console client for the SpaceWars coding challenge. I won the competition with this project.

The key was figuring out the positions of all enemies. When we got access to the project, nobody seemed to find an easy endpoint where we could get the game state. Many people tried to write just simple skill bots, and for some, it kind of worked, but nobody else did what I did, which was to inspect the game page/hud that the server gave you, and I realized that I could scrape the page using a web-scraping library and a little injected javascript. Got me the positions like a charm, and I used that and some simple trig maths to build an aimbot. Needless to say, I blew all of my competition away when I had the ability to just point and shoot with one press of a button, after that, it was pretty simple, defeat a few enemies, buy the farthest-range and most damaging weapon in the game, and just unleash the full potential of the bot, and wipe out the rest of the competition with 10x the score of the next player down.
